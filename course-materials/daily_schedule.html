Daily cadence: Reading Assignments are due the same class day the topic is introduced (pre-class check). Daily Assignments are due before the start of the next class. Complex Problems are assigned chapter-wise (not per day): each chapter's Complex Problems are uploaded in Moodle by the end of the following week's Sunday (11:59 PM), with the accompanying write-up due in class the next class meeting after that. The Weekly Check-In Quiz is due at the beginning of class on the first day of each week, and Concept Quest is due biweekly on the last day of the week. Due dates automatically skip forward over breaks (Labor Day, Community Engagement Day, mid-term break, Thanksgiving) to the next real class meeting. Instructor note: the final chapter (Sound) is a scheduling exception, since no class meetings remain after its usual due-date cycle; its Moodle deadline is pulled back a week so the write-up can still be submitted at the Final Exam Review session.

Chapter 5 Assignment: Newton's Laws and Applications

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 13–16

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 13

(a) A 65 kg astronaut pushes off a 550 kg spacecraft with a force of 320 N for 0.4 s. Find the astronaut's resulting velocity. (Give your answer in m/s.)

(b) Same push: find the spacecraft's resulting velocity. (Give your answer in m/s.)

HW-HARD Q2Day 13 A 0.6 kg ball is caught, decelerating from 22 m/s to 0 in 0.12 s. Find the average force exerted by the hand. (Give your answer in N.)
HW-HARD Q3Day 14

(a) A 4 kg block on a 22 degree frictionless incline is connected over a pulley at the top to a hanging 6 kg block. Find the acceleration. (Give your answer in m/s^2.)

(b) Same system: find the tension. (Give your answer in N.)

HW-HARD Q4Day 15 A 12 N force is applied to a block on a frictionless incline such that its parallel and perpendicular components are equal. Find the required angle. (Give your answer in degrees.)
HW-HARD Q5Day 15

(a) A frictionless conveyor inclined at 22 degrees must decelerate a 7 kg package moving up the incline, initial speed 3.2 m/s. Find the deceleration. (Give your answer in m/s^2.)

(b) Same package: find the stopping distance. (Give your answer in m.)

HW-HARD Q6Day 16

(a) A 38 kg skier is on a 36 degree slope with mu_k=0.09. Find the acceleration. (Give your answer in m/s^2.)

(b) Same skier: find the speed after sliding 28 m from rest. (Give your answer in m/s.)

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 13

An elevator (mass 900 kg, including passengers) accelerates upward at 1.8 m/s² for the first 2 s of its trip, then travels at constant velocity for 6 s, then decelerates to a stop over the final 1.5 s. Find the tension in the supporting cable during each of the three phases, and find the passenger's apparent weight (normal force from the floor) during the acceleration phase for a 70 kg passenger.

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 14

An Atwood-style rescue system lowers a 70 kg worker (hanging) while counterbalanced by an 85 kg weight over a frictionless pulley. Find the system's acceleration and the tension in the rope, then find how long it would take the worker to descend 15 m from rest, connecting back to kinematics.

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 15

A 22 kg crate slides down a frictionless 28° ramp starting from rest. Find its acceleration, its speed after sliding 4 m, and the time this takes (connecting to kinematic equations).

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 16

A 1400 kg car traveling at 90 km/h on a wet road (muk=0.35) brakes to a stop. Convert the speed to m/s, find the stopping distance, and compare it to the stopping distance on a dry road (muk=0.7) at the same initial speed.

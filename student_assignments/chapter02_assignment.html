Chapter 2 Assignment: One-Dimensional Kinematics

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 4–7

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 4

(a) An object's velocity is 1 m/s at t=0, 7 m/s at t=4 s, and 4 m/s at t=8 s. Find the average acceleration over the full 0-8 s interval. (Give your answer in m/s^2.)

(b) Same data: find the average acceleration over the first half (0-4 s). (Give your answer in m/s^2.)

(c) Same data: find the average acceleration over the second half (4-8 s). (Give your answer in m/s^2.)

HW-HARD Q2Day 4

(a) A migrating bird flies 240 km south over 5 hours, then 80 km north over 2 hours. Find its average velocity for the entire flight (north positive). (Give your answer in km/h.)

(b) Same flight: find the average speed for the entire flight. (Give your answer in km/h.)

HW-HARD Q3Day 5 A velocity-time graph consists of four linear segments with different slopes. Describe how you would calculate the total displacement and identify the time of maximum speed. (Upload your sketch/explanation.)
HW-HARD Q4Day 6 Two trains 450 m apart approach each other, one at constant 18 m/s and the other decelerating from 22 m/s at 2.5 m/s^2. Find the time at which they meet. (Give your answer in s.)
HW-HARD Q5Day 6

(a) An object decelerating uniformly covers 40 m in the first 3 s of braking and a total of 55 m before stopping. Find the initial velocity. (Give your answer in m/s.)

(b) Same object: find the deceleration. (Give your answer in m/s^2.)

HW-HARD Q6Day 7

(a) A ball is thrown straight up at 20 m/s from a rooftop 18 m above the ground. Find the maximum height above the ground. (Give your answer in m.)

(b) Same ball: find the total flight time until it lands on the ground below. (Give your answer in s.)

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 4

A delivery drone flies 3.2 km north at a reported speed of "45 km/h," then 1.8 km south at "60 mph." Convert both speeds to consistent SI units, find the time for each leg, and compute the drone's average velocity (magnitude and direction) for the entire trip.

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 5

A student computes an object's "instantaneous velocity" at t=2 s by finding the average velocity between t=0 s and t=4 s (a 4 s window centered on t=2 s), arguing this is "close enough." Evaluate this method, using the position function x(t)=t³ as a specific test case: compute the true instantaneous velocity at t=2 s via differentiation, then compute the student's average-velocity approximation, and discuss the size and cause of the discrepancy.

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 6

Two trains 850 m apart on the same track approach each other: Train A at a constant 72 km/h, Train B decelerating from 90 km/h at 1.5 m/s². Convert all speeds to SI units, then determine whether and when the trains meet, given emergency braking begins simultaneously on both at t=0 (assume they can pass safely if they never occupy the same position).

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 7

A construction worker drops a bolt from a scaffold 45 m above the ground at the same instant a coworker on the ground throws a safety cone straight up at 12 m/s from directly below. Determine whether and when the bolt and the cone collide, and find the height at which this occurs.

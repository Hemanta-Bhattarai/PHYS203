Chapter 3 Assignment: Vectors

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 8–9

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 8

(a) A navigation vector is 12 km at a bearing of 080 degrees. Find the east component. (Give your answer in km.)

(b) Same vector: find the north component. (Give your answer in km.)

HW-HARD Q2Day 8 A boat's velocity relative to water is (5,0) m/s, current is (0,1.5) m/s. Find the boat's speed relative to ground. (Give your answer in m/s.)
HW-HARD Q3Day 8

(a) A hiker walks (1.5 km,0deg), (2.5 km,90deg), (1 km,240deg). Find the net displacement's magnitude. (Give your answer in km.)

(b) Same trip: find the net displacement's direction in degrees. (Give your answer in degrees.)

HW-HARD Q4Day 9 A box is pulled up a 20 degree incline by a rope at 28 degrees above the surface with tension 110 N, while friction of 35 N acts down the incline. Find the net parallel force. (Give your answer in N.)
HW-HARD Q5Day 9 A 30 kg cart on an 18 degree incline is held stationary by a horizontal force. Find the required force. (Give your answer in N.)
HW-HARD Q6Day 9 A conveyor belt inclined at 22 degrees carries a 4 kg package. Find the minimum coefficient of static friction needed.

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 8

A search-and-rescue helicopter flies three legs: 12 km at 30° north of east, then 8 km due south, then 5 km at 60° west of north. Find the helicopter's net displacement (magnitude and direction) from its starting point, then find its average velocity if the entire trip took 45 minutes (convert to consistent units).

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 8

A delivery drone's flight plan consists of four legs: 2.5 km east, 1.8 km at 40° north of east, 3.0 km north, and 1.2 km at 20° west of north. Find the drone's net displacement (magnitude and direction), and if the trip takes 20 minutes, find its average speed (using total path length) and average velocity (using net displacement) in m/s.

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 9

A wheelchair ramp rises 0.9 m over a horizontal run of 10.8 m. Find the incline angle, then find the parallel and perpendicular components of the weight of a 90 kg (combined chair + rider) system on this ramp (g=9.8 m/s²).

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 9

A ski jump's takeoff ramp is inclined at 38°. A 75 kg skier (including equipment) is momentarily at rest at the top of the ramp before beginning to move. Find the parallel and perpendicular components of the skier's weight on the ramp, and convert the parallel component's equivalent acceleration (parallel component / mass) to a multiple of g.

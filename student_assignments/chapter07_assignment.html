Chapter 7 Assignment: Work and Energy

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 19–21

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 19

(a) A box (8.5 kg) is pushed up a 22 degree incline for 5.5 m by a force of 65 N along the incline. Find the work done by the applied force. (Give your answer in J.)

(b) Same box: find the work done by gravity. (Give your answer in J.)

HW-HARD Q2Day 19

(a) A 5.5 kg object starts at rest with 210 J of net work done on it. Find its final speed. (Give your answer in m/s.)

(b) A 10.5 kg object also has 210 J of net work done on it. Find its final speed. (Give your answer in m/s.)

HW-HARD Q3Day 20

(a) A spring (k=460 N/m) is compressed 0.125 m to launch a toy. Find the elastic PE stored. (Give your answer in J.)

(b) Find the maximum height reached by a 0.055 kg toy. (Give your answer in m.)

HW-HARD Q4Day 20 A 3.5 kg ball rolls down a frictionless 8.5 m hill from rest. Find its speed at the bottom. (Give your answer in m/s.)
HW-HARD Q5Day 21

(a) A 62 kg skier starts from rest at the top of a 47 m frictionless slope, then a horizontal section with friction (mu_k=0.11). Find the speed at the bottom. (Give your answer in m/s.)

(b) Same skier: find the stopping distance. (Give your answer in m.)

HW-HARD Q6Day 21 A roller coaster loop (radius 11 m) requires minimum speed at the top. Find the minimum hill height needed. (Give your answer in m.)

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 19

A 1200 kg car accelerates from 10 m/s to 28 m/s over 120 m on a level road while overcoming 900 N of combined friction/air resistance. Find the net work done, the work done by friction, and the total (larger) applied engine force work required, using the work-energy theorem together with Newton's second law ideas from Chapter 5.

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 19

A 1350 kg car accelerates from 12 m/s to 30 m/s over 140 m while overcoming 950 N of resistance. Find the net work done and the required engine force work, and convert the net work to kilowatt-hours (a common energy billing unit) to comment on the scale of energy involved compared to household electricity use.

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 20

A 0.4 kg ball is launched vertically by a spring (k=350 N/m) compressed 0.1 m, then continues upward under gravity alone after leaving the spring. Find the ball's speed as it leaves the spring, and find the total maximum height it reaches above the spring's natural (uncompressed) position.

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 21

A 60 kg skier starts from rest at the top of a 40 m frictionless slope, then crosses a horizontal section with friction (muk=0.09) before reaching a second, frictionless 15 m uphill slope. Find the skier's speed at the bottom of the first slope, the distance they travel on the horizontal section before needing to check whether they still have any speed left to start up the second slope, and (if they do) their speed at the top of the second slope, assuming a given horizontal-section length of 80 m.

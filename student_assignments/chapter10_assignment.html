Chapter 10 Assignment: Waves

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 30–32

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 30

(a) A guitar string vibrates at 440 Hz producing sound traveling through air (340 m/s). Find the sound wavelength in air. (Give your answer in m.)

(b) If the guitar string's wave has wavelength 1.15 m, find the wave speed on the string, and upload your work.

HW-HARD Q2Day 30

(a) A sound wave at 20,000 Hz travels at 343 m/s. Find its wavelength. (Give your answer in m.)

(b) Find the wavelength of a 20 Hz wave at the same speed. (Give your answer in m.)

HW-HARD Q3Day 31 Two speakers playing identical in-phase tones create a quiet spot at a listener location due to path-length-induced destructive interference. Explain why this occurs. Upload your written response.
HW-HARD Q4Day 31 Explain why ocean waves from two directions create both large waves and calm patches near a harbor entrance. Upload your written response.
HW-HARD Q5Day 32 An air column closed at one end has fundamental frequency 155 Hz. Find its length (speed of sound 340 m/s). (Give your answer in m.)
HW-HARD Q6Day 32 A string fixed at both ends has fundamental frequency 225 Hz and wave speed 280 m/s. Find the string's length. (Give your answer in m.)

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 30

A wave source vibrates as SHM with period 0.02 s (connecting to Chapter 9), generating a wave that travels through a medium at 340 m/s. Find the wave's frequency (using the SHM-period relationship) and its wavelength.

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 30

A wave source vibrates with period 0.025 s, generating a wave traveling at 320 m/s through its medium. Find the frequency and wavelength.

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 31

A noise-canceling headphone system detects ambient noise (amplitude A) and generates a canceling wave with the same frequency but only 92% of the amplitude, precisely out of phase. Find the resulting residual sound's amplitude as a fraction of the original noise amplitude, and (connecting to Day 34's decibel concept, previewed here) discuss qualitatively whether an 8% amplitude residual corresponds to an 8% reduction in perceived loudness.

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 32

A guitar string of length 0.66 m is tuned so its fundamental frequency is 329.6 Hz (the note E). Find the wave speed on the string, and find the frequency of the string's third harmonic, connecting to the harmonic-series concept.

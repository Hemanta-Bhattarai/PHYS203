Chapter 4 Assignment: Two-Dimensional Motion

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 10–12

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 10

(a) An object has vx(t)=4.5 (constant), vy(t)=8.5-4t. Find the time at which vy=0. (Give your answer in s.)

(b) Same object: find the speed at that instant. (Give your answer in m/s.)

HW-HARD Q2Day 10 Two particles start at the origin at the same time. Particle A has constant velocity (5.5,5.5) m/s. Particle B has velocity (11,0) m/s and acceleration (0,-4.2) m/s^2. Find the time(s), if any, at which they are at the same position. Upload your work.
HW-HARD Q3Day 11 A projectile launched at 45 degrees reaches a range of 120 m. Find the required launch speed. (Give your answer in m/s.)
HW-HARD Q4Day 11

(a) A golfer's shot has a time of flight of 4.5 s and a range of 160 m. Find the launch speed. (Give your answer in m/s.)

(b) Same shot: find the launch angle in degrees. (Give your answer in degrees.)

HW-HARD Q5Day 12 A roller coaster loop has radius 9 m. Find the minimum speed at the top such that riders' weight alone provides the centripetal force. (Give your answer in m/s.)
HW-HARD Q6Day 12 A centrifuge spins a sample at radius 0.08 m with centripetal acceleration 4500 m/s^2. Find the required speed at that radius. (Give your answer in m/s.)

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 10

A hovercraft's position is given by x(t) = 4t, y(t) = 6t - 0.5t² (meters, SI units) for a rescue maneuver. Find its velocity components as functions of time, its speed and direction of travel at t=3 s, and the time at which its y-velocity becomes zero.

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 10

A drone's position is x(t)=5t, y(t)=8t-0.6t² (meters). Find its velocity vector at t=2 s (magnitude and direction), and find the time at which the drone's path is purely horizontal (i.e., vy=0).

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 11

A fire hose nozzle launches water at 22 m/s at 35° above horizontal from a height of 1.5 m, aiming at a fire on a balcony 1.5 m above the ground and 30 m away horizontally. Determine whether the water stream's trajectory passes through that target point (height and horizontal distance simultaneously), showing your full reasoning.

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 12

A car (1400 kg) enters an unbanked highway curve of radius 120 m. The maximum static friction force the tires can provide is measured as 9800 N. Find the maximum safe speed for the curve, then convert this to km/h and compare it to a posted speed limit of 100 km/h, stating whether the limit is conservative or not for this curve.

Chapter 11 Assignment: Sound

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 33–35

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 33 A geologist measures a 47 s delay between P-waves (6.1 km/s) and S-waves (3.55 km/s). Find the distance to the epicenter. (Give your answer in km.)
HW-HARD Q2Day 33 A canyon echo takes 2.5 s to return at 18 degrees C. Find the distance to the canyon wall. (Give your answer in m.)
HW-HARD Q3Day 34 A construction site has four identical jackhammers each at 92 dB. Estimate the combined decibel level. (Give your answer in dB.)
HW-HARD Q4Day 34 Using the same source (82 dB at 10 m), find the decibel level at 40 m. (Give your answer in dB.)
HW-HARD Q5Day 35 A source moves toward an observer at 17 m/s while the observer moves toward the source at 11 m/s (source frequency 510 Hz). Find the observed frequency. (Give your answer in Hz.)
HW-HARD Q6Day 35 An observer moving toward a source at 22 m/s measures 530 Hz (speed of sound 340 m/s). Find the source's true frequency. (Give your answer in Hz.)

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 33

A ship's sonar sends a pulse that returns after 0.82 s, with sound traveling at 1500 m/s in seawater. Find the depth of the water beneath the ship, then (connecting to the wave equation from Day 30) find the wavelength of this sonar pulse if its frequency is 50 kHz.

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 33

A submarine's sonar pulse returns after 0.85 s at a speed of 1500 m/s in seawater. Find the distance to the detected object, and find the sonar pulse's wavelength if its frequency is 45 kHz.

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 34

A stadium's sound system produces 120 dB at 5 m from the speakers. Using the inverse-square law, estimate the decibel level a fan would experience 50 m away, then (connecting to Day 31's superposition ideas) discuss qualitatively how the presence of two identical speaker stacks, each independently producing this level, might combine at a given point — would it simply double the dB value?

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 35

A police car (siren 650 Hz) approaches a stationary pedestrian at 22 m/s, in air at 20°C. Find the speed of sound at this temperature (connecting to Day 33), then find the observed frequency during approach and (after the car passes) during recession.

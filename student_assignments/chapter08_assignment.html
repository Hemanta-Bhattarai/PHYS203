Chapter 8 Assignment: Rotational Motion

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 22–27

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 22

(a) A wheel decelerates from 32 rad/s to rest over 55 rad. Find the angular acceleration. (Give your answer in rad/s^2.)

(b) Same wheel: find the time taken. (Give your answer in s.)

HW-HARD Q2Day 23 A falling 6.5 kg mass is connected over a pulley with I=0.22 kg*m^2 and R=0.16 m. Find the speed after falling 4.2 m. (Give your answer in m/s.)
HW-HARD Q3Day 24 A rotating door: 27 N force perpendicular to it at 0.82 m from the hinge (opening), and a hinge friction torque of 11 N*m opposing motion. Find the net torque. (Give your answer in N*m.)
HW-HARD Q4Day 25

(a) A rotating platform (I=210 kg*m^2) has a person apply 52 N tangentially at radius 1.6 m, with a friction torque of 21 N*m opposing. Find the net torque. (Give your answer in N*m.)

(b) Same platform: find the angular acceleration. (Give your answer in rad/s^2.)

(c) Same platform, from rest: find the angular velocity after 6.2 s. (Give your answer in rad/s.)

HW-HARD Q5Day 26 A neutron star forms from a collapsing star (initial radius 720,000 km, angular velocity 1.1e-6 rad/s) collapsing to radius 11 km. Find the resulting angular velocity. (Give your answer in rad/s.)
HW-HARD Q6Day 27

(a) A bowling ball (M=7.2 kg, R=0.115 m) rolls at 4.7 m/s on a level alley. Find its total KE. (Give your answer in J.)

(b) Same ball: find the additional height it could climb on a frictionless ramp. (Give your answer in m.)

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 22

A washing machine's drum accelerates uniformly from rest to 1000 rpm over 8 s during spin-up. Convert to rad/s, find the angular acceleration, the number of full revolutions completed during spin-up, and the linear speed of a point on the drum's inner wall (radius 0.28 m) at the end of spin-up.

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 23

A solid cylindrical flywheel (mass 12 kg, radius 0.25 m) is connected via a string over a pulley (treat the pulley as massless here) to a hanging 4 kg mass. Find the system's speed after the hanging mass has fallen 1.5 m, using energy conservation that accounts for both translational and rotational kinetic energy.

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 25

A merry-go-round (I=280 kg·m²) at rest is pushed tangentially by two children, each applying 45 N at a radius of 1.8 m in the same rotational direction, while a 15 N·m frictional torque opposes the motion. Find the angular acceleration, then find the angular velocity after 5 s, and convert this to rpm.

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 26

A figure skater with I=5.5 kg·m² (arms extended) spins at 1.8 rad/s, then pulls in to I=2.2 kg·m². Find the new angular velocity, then find the ratio of final to initial rotational kinetic energy, and (connecting to Ch.7 energy ideas) explain physically where the "extra" kinetic energy comes from.

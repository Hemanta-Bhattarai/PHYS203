Chapter 9 Assignment: Oscillations

PHYS203 General Physics I · 10 problems (6 Homework-Hard + 4 Synthesis) · Days 28–29

Part A — Homework Hard Problems

HW-HARD Q1Day 28 An oscillator's position is one-third cycle out of phase with a reference oscillator of frequency 5.5 Hz. Find the time delay. (Give your answer in s.)
HW-HARD Q2Day 28

(a) A mass-spring system completes exactly 39 cycles in 13 s. Find its frequency. (Give your answer in Hz.)

(b) Same system: find its period. (Give your answer in s.)

(c) Same system: find the number of cycles in a full minute.

HW-HARD Q3Day 28

(a) A vibrating string completes 440 cycles in exactly 1 s. Find its period. (Give your answer in s.)

(b) Discuss the musical significance of the 440 Hz frequency (from Q7).

HW-HARD Q4Day 29

(a) A mass-spring system has a measured period of 1.15 s with k=155 N/m. Find the mass. (Give your answer in kg.)

(b) Same system: find the new period if the mass is doubled. (Give your answer in s.)

HW-HARD Q5Day 29 A mass-spring system has k=330 N/m and the same period as a pendulum of length 1.6 m. Find the mass. (Give your answer in kg.)
HW-HARD Q6Day 29

(a) A student measures a pendulum's period as 1.85 s, but the true period is 1.8 s. Find the percentage error. (Give your answer in percent.)

(b) Same scenario: find the resulting percentage error in a calculated length. (Give your answer in percent.)

Part B — Synthesis Problems

SYNTHESIS Q1Day 28

A mass oscillating in SHM completes exactly 45 cycles in 18 s. Find its frequency and period, then (connecting to the circular-motion analogy from this chapter) find the equivalent angular velocity ω=2π f that would describe a reference point moving in a circle with this same period.

SYNTHESIS Q2Day 28

An oscillator completes 48 cycles in 19 s. Find its frequency and period, and the equivalent circular-motion angular velocity.

SYNTHESIS Q3Day 29

A 2.2 kg mass hangs at rest from a vertical spring, stretching it 0.055 m. Find the spring constant using Hooke's law (equilibrium, connecting to Ch.5's force-balance methods), then find the period of oscillation if the mass is set into vertical SHM on this same spring.

SYNTHESIS Q4Day 29

A 2.4 kg mass hangs from a vertical spring, stretching it 0.06 m. Find the spring constant and the period of oscillation if set into vertical SHM.
